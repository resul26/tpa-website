The Commercial Bridge

Unlocking World-Class Fabrication Capacity for Global Infrastructure

Trans-Pacific Alliance (TPA) serves as the exclusive international commercial representative for China Grand (ASME U Certified), bringing elite manufacturing capability to major energy, mining, and industrial infrastructure projects across Latin America, Australia, and worldwide.

  • Massive Fabrication Scale — Direct commercial access to 40,000 tonnes per year of heavy structural steel, process piping, and pressure vessel manufacturing capacity across 131,000 square meters of shop floor space in Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Nantong.

Executive Quality & Technical Leadership

"At Trans-Pacific Alliance, we don't just understand quality — we built our company on it."

Founded by a British Senior QA/QC Specialist with over 20 years of hands-on execution across major international oil, gas, and mining capital projects, TPA provides unmatched technical governance at the commercial level.

As the dedicated international project arm for China Grand, Trans-Pacific Alliance provides direct factory access backed by top-tier international credentials:

  • NACE / AMPP CIP Level 3 Senior Coating Inspector
  • API 510 Pressure Vessel Inspector
  • API 570 Piping Inspector
  • CSWIP 3.2 Senior Welding Inspector
  • CSWIP RI Radiographic Interpretation
  • CSWIP AUT Automated Ultrasonic Testing Data Interpretation
