The Commercial Bridge

Unlocking World-Class Fabrication Capacity for Global Infrastructure

  • Massive Fabrication Scale — Direct commercial access to 40,000 tonnes per year of heavy structural steel, process piping, and pressure vessel manufacturing capacity across 131,000 square meters of shop floor space.

Executive Quality & Technical Leadership

"At Trans-Pacific Alliance, we don't just understand quality — we built our company on it."

Founded by a British Senior QA/QC Specialist with over 20 years of hands-on execution across major international oil, gas, and mining capital projects, TPA provides unmatched technical governance at the commercial level.

Trans-Pacific Alliance provides direct factory access backed by top-tier international credentials:

  • NACE / AMPP CIP Level 3 Senior Coating Inspector
  • API 510 Pressure Vessel Inspector
  • API 570 Piping Inspector
  • CSWIP 3.2 Senior Welding Inspector
  • CSWIP RI Radiographic Interpretation
  • CSWIP AUT Automated Ultrasonic Testing Data Interpretation
